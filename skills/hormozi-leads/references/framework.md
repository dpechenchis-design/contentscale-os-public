# HORMOZI $100M LEADS FRAMEWORK

**Source:** Alex Hormozi, "$100M Leads: How to Get Strangers to Want to Buy Your Stuff"

**Core Premise:** You're not getting enough leads because you're not advertising enough. Advertising = letting people know about the stuff you sell. Advertising always works; it's only a matter of efficiency.

**Two ways to grow any business:**
1. Get more customers
2. Make them worth more

This book = #1.

## SECTION II: GET UNDERSTANDING

### Lead Definitions
- **Lead** = person you can contact
- **Engaged Lead** = person who shows interest in your stuff
- Goal: get ENGAGED leads, not just leads

### Offers & Lead Magnets

**Core Offer** = your paid product/service

**Lead Magnet** = complete solution to a narrow problem, given for FREE
- Solves narrow problem > reveals bigger problem > core offer solves bigger problem
- Must be valuable enough to sell on its own

**3 Types of Lead Magnets:**
1. **Reveal Their Problem** (diagnose/audit/assessment)
2. **Samples & Trials** (try before you buy)
3. **One Step of Multi-Step Process** (give step 1 free, sell steps 2-10)

**4 Delivery Mechanisms:**
1. Software
2. Information
3. Services
4. Physical Products

**7 Steps to Create a Lead Magnet:**
1. Identify your dream customer's problem
2. Figure out how to solve it
3. Pick a delivery vehicle (software/info/services/physical)
4. Name it something compelling
5. Make it easy to consume
6. Make it lead to your core offer
7. Make it good enough to sell

### #1 WARM OUTREACH

**10-Step Process:**
1. Get your list (phone contacts, social media, email)
2. Pick a platform
3. Personalize your message
4. Reach out
5. ACA Framework: Acknowledge > Compliment > Ask
6. Use the Value Equation (dream outcome + perceived likelihood + minimize time/effort)
7. Start with free offer (do it for free to get proof)
8. Ask for referrals after delivering (Hinge Method)
9. Track everything
10. Follow up (100 reach outs/day)

**ACA Framework:**
- **A**cknowledge = context for why you're reaching out
- **C**ompliment = something genuine about them
- **A**sk = your offer or question

**Hinge Method for Referrals:** After delivering value, ask "Do you know anyone else who might benefit from this?"

**Benchmarks:** 100 reach outs > ~20 replies > ~4 take free > ~1 converts

### #2 POST FREE CONTENT

**Content Unit = Hook + Retain + Reward**

**HOOK (3 components):**

1. **Topics** (5 categories):
   - Lessons from wins
   - Lessons from losses
   - Contrarian/controversial takes
   - How-to/tutorials
   - Lists/resources

2. **Headlines** (7 components):
   - Callout (who it's for)
   - Benefit (why they should care)
   - Timeframe
   - Container (format: list, steps, secrets)
   - Credibility
   - Specificity
   - Emotion

3. **Format** = platform-specific packaging

**RETAIN via:**
- Lists (easy to follow)
- Steps (actionable)
- Stories (engaging)

**REWARD:**
- Give:Ask ratio (give way more than you ask)
- Integrated offers (woven into content) vs Intermittent offers (standalone CTAs)

**7 Lessons From Making Content:**
1. Warm audience is your unfair advantage
2. More content = more reach
3. Quality improves with quantity
4. Give away the secrets; sell the implementation
5. Content compounds over time
6. Repurpose across platforms
7. Post consistently, not perfectly

### #3 COLD OUTREACH

**3 Problems Strangers Create (and solutions):**
1. They don't know you > Build trust through personalization
2. They don't trust you > Offer big fast value
3. They didn't ask for it > Use volume (most say no)

**Build a List (3 methods):**
1. Software tools (scrape/buy lists)
2. Manually build from directories/social
3. Use agencies/VAs

**Personalize + Big Fast Value:**
- Reference something specific about them
- Offer value before asking for anything
- Make it feel 1-to-1

**Volume (3 methods):**
1. Hire people to do outreach
2. Use software to automate
3. Combine both

**Benchmarks:**
- Phone: 100 calls > 20 pickups > 2-3 interested
- Email: 100 emails > 20 opens > 2-3 replies
- DM: 100 DMs > 30 reads > 3-5 replies

### #4 RUN PAID ADS

**Part I: Ad Content = Call Out + Value + CTA**

**Call Out Types:**

*Verbal:*
- **Labels** ("Attention gym owners...")
- **Yes-Questions** ("Tired of...")
- **If-Then** ("If you [situation], then [benefit]")
- **Ridiculous Results** (show extreme outcomes)

*Nonverbal:*
- **Contrast** (before/after, unexpected visuals)
- **Likeness** (look like your audience)
- **Mascots** (recognizable character)

**Part II: Math & Scaling**
- **ROAS** = Return on Ad Spend
- **LTV:CAC** = Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost ratio
- Target: LTV:CAC of 3:1 minimum (decent); 5:1+ (great)
- **Client Financed Acquisition** = front-end offer covers ad costs; profit comes from backend
- Testing: change one variable at a time; scale what works
- Scaling: increase budget gradually; expand to new audiences/platforms

---

### CORE FOUR ON STEROIDS: MORE > BETTER > NEW

**Step 1: MORE**
- Do way more of what's already working until it "breaks" (diminishing returns)
- Rule of 100: do 100 primary actions per day for 100 days

**Step 2: BETTER**
- Identify the constraint (what broke under volume)
- Fix it (improve copy, targeting, offer, process)
- Then go back to MORE

**Step 3: NEW**
- New Placements (same platform, different ad spots)
- New Platforms (expand to additional platforms)
- New Core Four method (add a second advertising method)

**Size of the Pie Fallacy:** People think the market is fixed. It's not. Your competition is not other businesses; it's your customer's ignorance. The pie grows as you advertise.

### #1 CUSTOMER REFERRALS

**Referral Growth Equation:**
% Clients Referred Monthly - % Clients Churned Monthly = % Monthly Compounding Growth

**Three scenarios:**
- Referrals > churn = exponential growth
- Referrals = churn = flat
- Referrals < churn = decline

**Two Reasons You Don't Get Referrals:**
1. Product isn't good enough
2. You don't ask

**Goodwill = Value - Price** (Goodwill drives word of mouth)

**6 Ways to Get More Referrals (by giving more value):**

1. **Sell Better Customers** (target ideal-fit; they get better results + refer more)
2. **Set Better Expectations** (underpromise, overdeliver)
3. **Get More People Better Results** (6-step process: survey best customers > interview > find common actions > force new customers to repeat > measure > match guarantee)
4. **Make Faster Wins** (5 ways: deliver at intervals, share progress, force wins in first 48h, BAMFAM, add 50% to timelines and deliver early)
5. **Keep Making Your Stuff Better** (6-step loop: find problem > figure fix > improve > test small > feedback > roll out or iterate)
6. **Tell Them What to Buy Next** (keep selling backend; don't neglect existing customers)

**"One Question to Rule Them All":** Imagine you have ONE customer and can ONLY grow through referrals. What would you change about your product?

**7 Ways to Ask for Referrals:**

1. **One-Sided Referral Benefit** (pay your CAC to referrer or friend)
2. **Two-Sided Referral Benefits** (split CAC between referrer + friend; Dropbox/PayPal model)
3. **Ask Right When They Buy** (on contract/checkout; ask WHO not IF)
4. **Referrals as Negotiation Chip** (discount in exchange for introductions)
5. **Referral Events** (time-limited "Bring-a-friend" / "Spouse Challenge" promotions)
6. **Ongoing Referral Programs** (talk about benefits of doing things together constantly; 33% boost)
7. **Unlockable Referral Bonuses** (VIP bonuses for refer + testimonial)

**Creative combo:** Gift card strategy (1/3 your cost; 7-14 day expiry; personalized with friend's name)

**Pro Tip:** Match referral incentive to your core product

### #2 EMPLOYEES

**How Employees Work:** Lead-getting employees use the Core Four on your behalf. Trade 40h doing for 4h managing = 36h saved; repeat at scale.

**Why They Make You Wealthy:**
- Business that runs without you = valuable asset
- "You get rich from what you make. You become wealthy from what you own."
- With you doing everything: business = income only
- Without you: business = sellable asset worth multiples of profit

**Mindset shifts:**
- "If you want it done right, get someone to spend ALL their time doing it."
- "If I can do it, someone else can do it better."
- "Everyone is replaceable, especially me."

**Internal Core Four (hiring = advertising for employees):**
- Warm Outreach > Asking Your Network
- Cold Outreach > Recruiting
- Post Content > Posting Job Openings
- Paid Ads > Promoting Job Postings
- + Employee Referrals, Associations/Guilds, Staffing Firms

**3D Training Method:**
1. **Document** (make a checklist)
2. **Demonstrate** (you do it in front of them)
3. **Duplicate** (they do it in front of you)

**Pro Tip:** Give short windows (3 weeks) to prove themselves

**Training rules:** Praise right steps; don't punish mistakes during training; one piece of feedback at a time; retrain on performance dips

**Calculating Returns:**
Total Payroll / Total Engaged Leads = cost per engaged lead > compare to LTGP:CAC

**Diagnostic question:** "Do my engaged leads have the problem I solve AND the money to spend?"
- No = advertising problem
- Yes but not buying = sales problem
- Don't fire sales for advertising problems or vice versa

### #3 AGENCIES

**Right way to use agencies:** Use them to LEARN, not to outsource forever

**Use agencies for:**
1. Learning new methods
2. Learning new platforms

**Agreement Framework:** "I want to do what you do but I don't know how. Work with me for 6 months so I can learn. I'll pay extra for you to explain your decisions. Then I'll train my team. Then switch to consulting."

**Two-agency strategy:** One "good enough" agency + one elite agency (at 4x price) for deeper learning. Compare your team's results to agency until yours beats theirs > switch to consulting > cut loose.

**10 Criteria for Picking the Right Agency:**
1. Referred by someone with good results
2. Prominent companies got results with them
3. Waiting list
4. Clear sales process with realistic expectations
5. Long-term strategy focus, no short-term hacks
6. Clear about what they need from you
7. Suggest regular meetings
8. Clear tracking/reporting
9. Good offer (evaluated via Value Equation)
10. Expensive (all good agencies are expensive)

**6 Next Steps:**
1. Decide if agency makes sense now
2. Talk to many agencies
3. Use agreement framework
4. Set deadline to learn skills
5. Use both teams until yours wins
6. Switch to consulting then cut loose

### #4 AFFILIATES & PARTNERS

**How Affiliates Work:** Independent business that tells their audience to buy your stuff, in exchange for money, free stuff, or both.

**Why You Want An Affiliate Army:**
- Each affiliate = new compounding revenue stream
- Selling customers: linear growth (10/mo = $100K/mo plateau)
- Selling affiliates: compounding growth (10 affiliates/mo, each bringing 1 customer = exponential)

**6 Steps to Build an Affiliate Army:**

**Step 1: Find Your Ideal Affiliate**
- Business with warm audience full of your potential customers
- Questions: What do they buy? Where do they go? What do they like to do?
- Categories: softwares, products, equipment, services, groups, events

**Step 2: Make Them an Offer**
- Call Out (their business type, their customers, results, products)
- Elements of Value (dream outcome, perceived likelihood, effort/sacrifice, time delay)
- CTA

**Step 3: Qualify Them (get them invested)**
Two ways:
1. **Make Them A Customer** (buy your product to maintain affiliate status; bulk purchases)
2. **Make Them An Expert** (pay for certification/onboarding; charge 10-20% of avg first-year affiliate earnings)

**Step 4: Figure Out What to Pay Them**
- What: new customers + repeat customers
- How much: based on max allowable CAC
- **3-Tier Payout Structure:**
  - Tier 1 = 25% of CAC (agree to try)
  - Tier 2 = 50% of CAC (activate/start selling)
  - Tier 3 = 100% of CAC (sustain/hit targets)
- Blended payout < max CAC = better margins
- Pro Tip: Pay with product ("Sell 3 Get It Free")

**Step 5: Get Them Advertising (LAUNCH)**
- **Whisper-Tease-Shout Method:**
  - **Whisper** (Call Outs): curiosity, mystery, behind-the-scenes. Start 4-6 weeks before, increase frequency
  - **Tease** (Elements of Value): reveal product, show date, demonstrate value. Start once/week until 14 days out, then twice/week
  - **Shout** (Call to Action): specific actions, bonuses, scarcity, urgency. Start 3 days out, increase to every 30 min on launch day

**Step 6: Keep Them Advertising (INTEGRATION)**
Three methods (easiest to hardest):
1. **Affiliates Give Away Your Lead Magnet** with their core offer > you upsell your core offer
2. **Affiliates Sell Your Lead Magnet** to their audience > they keep money > you upsell core offer
3. **Affiliates Sell Your Core Offer** directly > split upfront cash, all cash for period, or all cash forever

**Pro Tip: Employees Are Leads Too** - Companies that hire lots of people make great affiliates (employee perks = free leads)

**Pro Tip: White Label Lead Magnets** for affiliates to brand as their own

**Costs & Returns:**
- Don't compare LTGP:CAC of customers directly
- Compare: cost to get an affiliate vs gross profit of ALL customers that affiliate brings
- Target: LTGP:CAC of affiliates at 3:1 minimum; 5:1+ = scale aggressively
- **3 ways to improve ratio:**
  1. Lower CAC (better ads, offer, sales process)
  2. Increase LTGP & Decrease CAC (better launch/activation)
  3. Increase LTGP (better integration process)

**Three Case Studies:**
1. **Tax Prep Services ($50M):** Partners with entrepreneur trainers; free LLC setup as lead magnet; upsells bookkeeping/tax; zero paid ads
2. **Prestige Labs (supplements):** Gym Launch sells gym owners; introduces them to Prestige Labs; gyms sell supplements to members (4000 gyms x 14 sales/mo x $200)
3. **Chiropractors:** Partner with gyms; 3-hour posture workshop ($29-$99); gym keeps 100% of ticket revenue; chiropractor gets new patients; long-term integration = adjustments bundled with gym memberships

**Conclusion:** Treat affiliates like customers. Two ways to create a compounding business: find people who never stop buying (referrals) or find people who never stop selling for you (affiliates).

## SECTION V: GET STARTED

### Chapter 1: Advertising in Real Life (Open To Goal)

**The One Big Rule:** Volume. Most people dramatically underestimate the volume it takes to make advertising work.

**Rule of 100:** Do 100 primary actions per day, for 100 days
- 100 reach outs/day (outreach)
- 100 minutes of content/day (content)
- 100 dollars of ads/day (paid ads)
- Bonus: do 100/day of MORE than one Core Four

**Rule of 100 on Steroids: Open To Goal**
- Don't commit to a number of ACTIONS; commit to a number of OUTCOMES
- Work until you hit your daily goal, no matter how long it takes
- Example: gym chain required 5 new members/day; if done by lunch, go home; if not, work 18 hours

**3 High-ROI Habits:**
1. Wake up early (4-5am)
2. Get right to work (no rituals/routines)
3. No meetings until noon (focused work time)

**One Page Advertising Checklist (5 Steps):**
1. Pick the type of engaged lead: Customers, Affiliates, Employees, or Agencies
2. Pick Rule of 100 or Open To Goal; commit to daily advertising actions
3. Fill out the Advertising Daily Checklist (Who/What/Where/To Whom/When/Why/How/How Much/How Many/How Long)
4. Do this daily until you can afford to pay someone else to do it
5. Go back to Step 1; make employees your new target lead type; repeat 1-4 until fully staffed; then scale again

## KEY MENTAL MODELS

- **Advertising always works; it's only a matter of efficiency.** So once you start, keep going until it works.
- **You either win or you learn.** There is no losing unless you quit.
- **The Many Sided Die:** Keep rolling. Every green (win) turns a red side green, improving your odds forever. The game ends only when you stop rolling.
- **You cannot lose if you do not quit.**
- **Open To Goal > Rule of 100.** Don't commit to actions; commit to outcomes.
- **Set aside some percentage of your ad budget to try new things without expecting return.** Consider it an investment in your education.
- **Effort will never be the reason something doesn't work for you.** Everything else can fail; your volume won't.
