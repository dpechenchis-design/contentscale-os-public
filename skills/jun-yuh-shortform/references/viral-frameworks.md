## FRAMEWORK 0: BTS / SILENT VISUAL
**Stats:** 28 reels | Avg 2.16M views | Top 25M views
**Share of total views:** ~63%

**What it is:** No narration, pure visual montage set to trending music. Personal moments, filming process, travel, daily life.

**Structure:**
1. Opening shot — cinematic moment or emotional photo with text overlay
2. Montage — 8-15 clips, each 1-3 seconds, rhythm-synced to music
3. Text overlays — timestamps, short emotional phrases, context
4. Closing — usually a smile, achievement moment, or emotional payoff

**Hooks that work:**
- Personal timeline with year stamps ("2013: my mom left for Korea without a goodbye")
- Archival childhood photos (sometimes with black bar over eyes for privacy)
- Cinematic B-roll of daily life, filming process, travel

**When to use:** Need maximum reach. Have good footage. Trending audio available. Want to grow followers fast.

**Production notes:**
- Trending music is mandatory — this format lives or dies by audio selection
- Text overlays are minimal but emotionally loaded
- Warm, muted color grading (earth tones, soft lighting)
- Zero narration — the visuals and music carry everything

## FRAMEWORK 1: "X OUT OF X" ESCALATING LIST
**Stats:** 3 reels | Avg 1.08M views | Top 1.99M views

**What it is:** "X habits to become disgustingly [adjective], increasingly more niche." Each item escalates in specificity and emotional depth.

**Structure:**
1. Hook — "10/10 habits to become disgustingly [overeducated/disciplined/etc.]"
2. Items 1-3 — Broad, relatable habits anyone would agree with
3. Items 4-7 — Getting more specific, surprising, showing depth
4. Items 8-10 — Deeply personal, niche, emotionally resonant
5. The word "increasingly more niche" signals the escalation to the audience

**Hooks that work:**
- "10/10 habits to become disgustingly overeducated"
- "X things that changed my life, increasingly more niche"
- The word "disgustingly" is a Jun signature — implies obsessive depth

**Visual format:** Collage of aesthetic images above talking head. Grid of art/culture/book images signals intellectual depth.

**When to use:** Educational listicle content. Want to show depth and personality. Need to stand out from generic "5 tips" content.

**Key principle:** The escalation IS the format. If every item is at the same level, this framework fails. The audience stays for the deepening.

## FRAMEWORK 2: "SECRET FRAMEWORK" REVEAL
**Stats:** 9 reels | Avg 831K views | Top 2.76M views

**What it is:** Named systems with acronyms. The act of naming a framework makes it 2.3x more viral than unnamed teaching content (831K vs 365K avg).

**Named frameworks Jun has introduced:**
- **Storytelling Wheel (LIFE)** — Love, Identity, Fitness, Earnings — content topic generator
- **Silent Film Format** — Tell a story using only visuals and text, no narration
- **Grow Don't Gossip** — Focus energy on growth, not commentary on others
- **Picture Book Effect** — Each scene should be understandable as a single image, like a children's book
- **3 Whys** — Ask "why" three times to get to the emotional core of any story

**Structure:**
1. Hook — "The [Name] Framework that [impressive result]" or "Why every viral video uses [Name]"
2. Name reveal — State the framework name clearly, often with text overlay
3. Acronym/structure breakdown — Walk through each component
4. Example — Show how it applies to a real piece of content
5. CTA — "Save this" or "Try this on your next video"

**Hooks that work:**
- "The framework that got me 25M views"
- "Every viral storyteller uses this and doesn't know it"
- "I named this the [X] because..."

**Visual format:** Talking head with framework name as text overlay. Diagrams and visual elements appear as he explains.

**When to use:** Teaching any system or process. The "Name It to Claim It" rule applies — ALWAYS name your framework.

**Critical insight:** Framework 9 (unnamed teaching) averages 365K. This framework (named teaching) averages 831K. Same content depth. The name is worth 2.3x views.

## FRAMEWORK 3: PODCAST INTERVIEW TRAILER
**Stats:** 5 reels | Avg 699K views | Top 2.15M views

**What it is:** Rapid-fire guest soundbites from Jun's podcast, edited into a trailer with a cliffhanger ending.

**Structure:**
1. Big stat or claim from guest — text overlay with number
2. 3-5 rapid soundbites — each 2-4 seconds, best moments
3. Cliffhanger — cut right before the biggest revelation
4. CTA — "Full episode link in bio" or "Part 2 tomorrow"

**Hooks that work:**
- "You grew 10 million followers in 14 months" (stat about the guest)
- Guest saying something controversial or surprising
- A question Jun asks that makes the guest pause

**Visual format:** "Creator Unplugged" branded header. Jun top, guest bottom (or side-by-side). Big stat text overlay.

**When to use:** Have podcast/interview content. Want to drive traffic to long-form. Guest has impressive credentials or stats.

**Production notes:**
- The trailer must stand alone as entertaining — not just a "teaser"
- Each soundbite should be independently interesting
- End on the most compelling unanswered question

## FRAMEWORK 4: LIVE FRAMEWORK DEMO
**Stats:** 3 reels | Avg 690K views | Top 1.09M views

**What it is:** Showing a framework working in real time on a real person. Proof over theory.

**Structure:**
1. Setup — "Let me show you how [framework] works"
2. Subject — Real person (girlfriend, friend, stranger) agrees to participate
3. Demo — Jun walks through the framework live, reactions are genuine
4. Result — The framework produces a visible, emotional, or surprising outcome
5. Callback — "This is why [framework] works — you just saw it"

**Best example:** Jun demos the "3 Whys" framework on his girlfriend. Asks "why" three times, each answer goes deeper, she gets emotional. The audience watches the framework work in real time.

**Visual format:** Timeline graphic with "bad" and "event" markers. Jun talking head at bottom. Story unfolds along the timeline.

**When to use:** Have a framework you can demonstrate live. The real-time proof is more convincing than explanation.

**Key principle:** The demo must be genuine. Staged demos lose the magic. The subject's real reactions are the content.

## FRAMEWORK 5: REVENUE MILESTONE MONOLOGUE
**Stats:** 5 reels | Avg 561K views | Top 824K views

**What it is:** "$10M in 15 months" + dad/immigrant story + numbered lessons. Business results anchored in personal narrative.

**Structure:**
1. Milestone hook — Specific number + timeframe ("$10M in 15 months")
2. Origin story — Brief personal context (immigrant family, starting from nothing)
3. Numbered lessons — 3-5 specific lessons learned, each tied to a story
4. Emotional anchor — One lesson connects to family/identity/sacrifice
5. Forward-looking close — What's next, or invitation to the audience

**Hooks that work:**
- "$10M in 15 months — here's what I learned"
- "From [humble origin] to [impressive result]"
- Specific numbers are mandatory — vague claims don't work

**Visual format:** Jun speaking to camera, sometimes on stage. Aspirational settings (conference, nice location). Text overlay with numbers.

**When to use:** Hit a business milestone. Want to share lessons without bragging. Need to connect achievement to personal story.

**Key principle:** The milestone is the hook, but the story is the content. Without the personal narrative, this is just a flex.

## FRAMEWORK 6: "MAKE ME A VIDEO" COACHING DEMO
**Stats:** 2 reels | Avg 522K views | Top 675K views

**What it is:** Someone asks Jun to make a video about their topic. He shows the wrong approach, then the right approach. Transformation in real time.

**Structure:**
1. Setup — Person states their topic/niche ("I'm a fitness coach")
2. Wrong version — Jun shows the generic, boring approach most people would take
3. Diagnosis — "Here's why that doesn't work" — specific, constructive critique
4. Right version — Jun rewrites/restructures using his frameworks
5. Reaction — The person's genuine reaction to seeing the transformation

**Hooks that work:**
- "Make me a video" (simple, direct)
- "Can you fix my content?"
- The contrast between wrong and right is the entertainment

**When to use:** Want to show coaching ability. Need to demonstrate value. Have a willing participant.

## FRAMEWORK 7: ENTERTAINING MICRO-CONTENT
**Stats:** 10 reels | Avg 487K views | Top 827K views

**What it is:** Tier rankings, satire, skits, quiz gut punch. Pure entertainment with embedded expertise.

**Structure (varies by sub-type):**

- **Skit:** Act out a scenario (creator who focuses on wrong things)
- **Quiz gut punch:** Ask a question, reveal answer that challenges assumptions

**When to use:** Need variety in content calendar. Want to show personality. Break up heavy educational content.

**Key principle:** Entertainment is the vehicle, expertise is the payload. Every piece still teaches something.

**Topics Jun uses:**

## FRAMEWORK 10: OBJECTION CRUSHER
**Stats:** 7 reels | Avg 358K views | Top 680K views

**What it is:** Take the audience's biggest objection and dismantle it. "I have nothing to offer" becomes a superpower reveal.

**Structure:**
1. State the objection — Use the audience's exact words ("I don't have anything unique")
2. Validate — "I get it. I felt the same way."
3. Reframe — Show why the objection is actually the answer
4. Reveal — The weakness IS the superpower
5. Proof — Example from Jun's own life or a client's

**Hooks that work:**
- "I have nothing to offer" (direct quote from audience)
- "If you think you're not ready, watch this"
- "The thing you're ashamed of is your biggest asset"

**When to use:** Audience has a recurring objection. Need to address doubt. Want to reframe limitation as advantage.

## STORYTELLING WHEEL — LIFE CONTENT ENGINE

Jun's proprietary framework for generating personal story content:

```
         LOVE
          |
   ───────┼───────
   |      |      |
IDENTITY──┼──FITNESS
   |      |      |
   ───────┼───────
          |
       EARNINGS
```

- **L — Love:** Relationships, girlfriend moments, family bonds, friendship
- **I — Identity:** Immigrant story, Korean-American experience, faith, self-discovery
- **F — Fitness:** Discipline routines, health, physical transformation, daily habits
- **E — Earnings:** Revenue milestones, business lessons, client results, financial growth

**How to use:** Rotate through quadrants. Each quadrant provides both BTS content (visual moments) and spoken content (story/framework). One week might be Love-focused BTS + Identity-focused framework reveal.
